A two-sentence horror story, written by a program. The story reads:

> My wife and I buried our stillborn son in the garden in 2019, and every spring since, a small handprint has appeared on the inside of his bedroom window. This morning the handprint was on the outside, and the soil in the garden was freshly turned.

The first sentence plants a quiet, grieving ritual — a handprint that returns every spring on the inside of the glass. The second sentence turns it: the print is now on the outside, and the garden soil is freshly turned, so what they buried is no longer inside at all.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the story to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set), deterministically, with no trailing newline.
